About
Vocals, instrumentations, and images were generated by various local generative models like Riffusion, Stable Diffusion, Bark, Chirp, MusicGen, Flux with a bit of tweaking in Python and tons of prompting and generations. Mixing and sampling were done in FL Studio. Audio refinements were done in Audition. Visuals were created, corrected and edited in Photoshop. Animations and motion graphics were created with After Effects with heavy reliance on audio keyframes. Video editing was done in Premiere Pro. All these songs have been downscaled and compressed with Handbrake to 500 pixels and 256 bitrate and in a mono channel. The uncompressed assets runs into 1000s of gigabytes. The UI/UX has been coded and created with JavaScript and Web Audio API. For some of the lyrics I did online researches to find the complete original sources and transliteration of them. As these lyrics are from various ancient literary sources from across the world, with a bit of focus on extinct languages. I have created a nice music player above to enjoy these songs. The background fractal responds to the audio amplitude. There is also a scrolling amplitude map. And please wait if the internet or the device speed is slow, the songs might take time to load.
Interface
Play: Play or resumes the current displayed song.
Previous: Move to the previous song in the sequence and play it automatically.
Next: Move to the next song in the sequence and play it automatically.
Pause: Pause the current displayed song if playing.
Loop: Play the current displayed song on repeat.
Play All: Play all the song in the sequence automatically one by one starting from the current displayed song.
Stop: Stop the song and jump to the first song in the sequence.
Bass: Increases the bass successively on multiple press.
Treble: Increases the treble successively on multiple press.
Binaural: Changes the audio channel in a fixed duration from left to right to left on repeat.
Flat: Resets the Bass, Treble and Binaural to default zero.
Seek Bar: To seek to any part of the current displayed song.
Selector Dropdown: Choose and jump to any song in the sequence and play it automatically.
Thats All! 🙂
